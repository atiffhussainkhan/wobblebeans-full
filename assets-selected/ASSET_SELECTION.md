## What each repo contains
- **atiffhussainkhan/Image**: a catalogue/metadata repo of about 819 MB, with 1,760 webp preview atlases, scripts and CSV/JSONL indexes. Its `game-art-search-v2` index lists 114,852 images taken from 4 user-supplied clipart archives (licence "unverified"). The real packs are attached as **GitHub Release** zips (28 packs, mostly CC0: Kenney mirror, KayKit, Quaternius, and others). They hold both 2D and 3D art. LFS is configured but almost unused.
- **atiffhussainkhan/animation** (private): 19 files, about 464 KB. It holds **2D sprite sheets only**: OpenGameArt CC0 Knight/Princess/Dragon with Idle/Walk/Jump/Die (12 webp sheets, 132 frames, squashed 2:1 for an isometric canvas game), plus one Python build script and docs. There are **no 3D clips and no rig**, so nothing from it was picked (it is 2D, off-style, and has knight/sword/"Die" content).

## Rejected
- All clipart from the Image index. Reasons: unverified licences, dated 1990s/2003 style, trademarks/IP (a "Disney Clipart 800+" folder, Apple, Windows, Visa/Mastercard/Discover, Half-Life/Counter-Strike), a gun, a skull, a devil, a vampire, and photos of real people.
- Kenney saw, spike-block, trap-spikes and bomb (the spec bans spikes and violence). Particle fire, flame, muzzle, scorch, slash and scratch (violent or harsh).
- KayKit/Quaternius attack, shoot, block, punch, sword and death clips. Kenney mini-characters, blocky-characters and mini-arena (humans and weapons).
- The animation repo's sprite sheets (reason above).

## Gaps (still need to be made or found)
1. **No real bean character and no bean rig** using the spec bones (root>pelvis>spine_01>spine_02>head + arms/legs). Prototype Pete and the Kenney blobs are placeholders only.
2. **No modular costumes** (top/bottom halves, hats as rigid attachments). There are also no faceplates, eye/mouth decals or blend shapes.
3. **Animations**: every clip needs retargeting to the bean rig. KayKit uses rigid parts (Head/arms/Body nodes); Quaternius uses a humanoid Hips/Torso armature. The library is missing these clips: dedicated grab_hold/pull, dive airborne, belly slide, getup_front/back, ledge mantle (Climbing is only approximate), stumble/knockback, fall loop, land, respawn, thumbs-up, and lower/upper-body layer splits.
4. **VFX**: no confetti and no 3D/mesh puffs. The particles are greyscale and need tinting.
5. **UI**: no banners/nameplates and no game font. Icons are 100 px white glyphs (taken from the 2x folder) rather than the 256² source spec. Medals (41×74) and the candy tile set (70 px) are too low-res for final use.
6. **Obstacles**: no sweeper/spinner bar or bumper arm (substitutes: minigolf windmill, door-rotate), no fan/wind, no slime/goo floor, and no giant soft balls.
7. Skyboxes are not cubemaps (2:1 panoramas), and there is no Frosty-specific sky.
