# Wobblebeans audio

Every sound in this folder is original and procedural. Oscillators, noise buffers, and biquad filters are scheduled by the Web Audio graph in `synth.ts`. There are no sampled recordings, no voice-over, and no stems from another game.

- Author: Wobblebeans workstream 6
- Licence: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- AI-use: none. The synthesisers were written by hand.
- Provenance record: `assets/licenses/wobblebeans_audio_PROVENANCE.md`

A scored music bed can replace the procedural loops later. It has to be CC0 (or otherwise licensed for a children's game) and it has to land under `assets/audio/` with its own licence file. Do not drop in commercial packs or reference-game stems.
